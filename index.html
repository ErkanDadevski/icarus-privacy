2. Summary

Icarus does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game works completely offline.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following information locally on your device so that it can work as expected:

  • your highscore,
  • your settings (music and sound volume, mute options),
  • whether you have already seen the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

4. What the game does not do

  • It does not request internet access.
  • It does not use accounts or logins.
  • It does not show advertisements.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not include third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Google Play

If you install the game through Google Play, Google may process data about your installation and device according to its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We have no influence on and no access to this data, other than the aggregated statistics Google provides to developers.

6. Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your rights

Since we do not process personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have questions about this policy or your rights under applicable data protection law (such as the GDPR), you can contact us at the address above.

8. Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example by adding online features), this policy will be updated before such a version is released. The current version is always available at this address.
